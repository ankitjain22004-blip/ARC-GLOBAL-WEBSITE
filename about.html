Our story

ARC Global was founded in 2016 with a mission to bridge classroom education and real-world engineering. It has grown into one of India’s most trusted names in prototype vehicle development, engineering consulting and advanced mobility.

We are India’s complete student motorsports engineering and manufacturing solutions company: we design, engineer, manufacture, assemble and test vehicles, then support the teams that race them.

We don’t simply manufacture vehicles.
We build engineers.
